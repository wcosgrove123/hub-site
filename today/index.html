Section

The Market Revolution

Question of the day WXT

When a machine saves labor, who gets the savings?

The standing question in today's words: does new technology spread power or concentrate it?

Warm-up 4 min

Predict in pencil: for the cotton gin, the Lowell mill, the Erie Canal, and the telegraph, who did it help most: owner, worker, farmer, or consumer? And who paid? Commit on all four; the stations check your guesses.

By the end of today you can

  • Explain the causes and effects of the innovations in technology, agriculture, and commerce, 1800–1848 (CED 4.5).
  • Evaluate who gained and who paid for one innovation, using one piece of evidence from a station (CED 4.6).
