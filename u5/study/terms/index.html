310 terms

  • national culture

    1800–1848

    What it was
    A new American culture that mixed American subjects, European influences and regional styles.
    Why it matters
    Writers and painters set out to show what was distinctly American.
    ARC NAT idea Class D4–D7 pods Named in the CED

  • Romanticism

    1820s–1850s

    What it was
    Movement that prized emotion, nature, intuition and the individual over Enlightenment reason.
    Why it matters
    Fed the belief that people and society could be perfected, which drove reform.
    ARC movement Class D4–D7 pods Named in the CED

  • human perfectibility

    1820s–1850s

    What it was
    Romantic belief that people, and society with them, could be improved and even perfected.
    Why it matters
    Gave reformers confidence that drinking, ignorance, cruelty and slavery could be ended.
    ARC SOC idea Class D4 Named in the CED

  • Hudson River School

    1825–1870s

    What it was
    Painters such as Thomas Cole who made the American wilderness a national subject.
    Why it matters
    Treated the landscape as a source of national pride, much as Manifest Destiny did.
    ARC GEO movement Reading A CED example
