Presidents

8 presidents, 1837 to 1861.

These 8 presidents held the office from 1837 to 1861. Two of them died in office, Harrison after a month and Taylor after 16 months, and the vice presidents who took their places, Tyler and Fillmore, were never elected president. Tyler and Taylor are the two who get mixed up: Tyler was the vice president who took over in 1841, and Taylor won the election of 1848.
