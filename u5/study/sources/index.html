36 primary sources.

HAPP is the four things to say about a source: its historical situation, its audience, its purpose and its point of view. Try the HAPP yourself first. Then open the worked one and compare.

  • John L. O'Sullivan, “Annexation,” The United States Magazine and Democratic Review 17 (July–August 1845): 5–10

    editorial · 1845 · Topic 5.2

    Check your HAPP
    H Historical situation
    Texas annexation had just passed; Mexico objected and Oregon was in dispute with Britain.
    A Audience
    Democratic readers and politicians.
    P Purpose
    To defend annexing Texas and argue that U.S. expansion across the continent was destined.
    P Point of view
    A Democratic editor who saw American growth as God-given.
