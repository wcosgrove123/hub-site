The Case Method: Cherokee Removal

Question of the day · SOC

Who decides who is a nation?

The Cherokee wrote a constitution in 1827. Georgia said there was no such nation inside Georgia. Somebody has to decide.

The question's shape: continuity and change.

Contextualization 4 min

The timeline strip from 10/9 with the Georgia row added:

  • 1802 Compact (the U.S. promises Georgia it will end Indian title)
  • 1827 Cherokee constitution
  • 1828 the Phoenix, Jackson elected, gold in north Georgia
  • 1828–1830 Georgia extends its laws over Cherokee land
  • May 1830 Indian Removal Act. Context sentence modeled aloud.

Content 8 min

Model, thinking aloud (I do). The four case moves on a one-paragraph mini-case the class has not seen: Georgia and the Creek lands, 1827 (a governor surveys treaty land and dares the president to stop him; what should J. Q. Adams do?). 1 Find the decision: who must decide what, by when. 2 Map the actors: what each wants and what each can do. 3 List the options and the evidence for each. 4 Take a position you can defend from the text, and do not look up the ending. Pods vote; only then reveal what Adams did.

  • wants
  • can do
  • page).

Closure 5 min

Exit ticket scored 0 to 3 against the criteria on the board: the decision (1), two actors with what each wants (1), one question the case has raised for you (1). Last line: rate yourself against the criteria and say what you need tomorrow. Back to the pre-assessment: what would you add to your two sentences?
