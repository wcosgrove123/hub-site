Contextualization

Yesterday's board, still up.

Content 8 min

The reveal, three slides. 1 May 1836: the Senate ratifies by a single vote, over a protest petition carrying almost 16,000 Cherokee names. 2 1838: the army under Winfield Scott rounds up those who stayed; the department's figures are about 15,000 removed and an estimated 4,000 dead (estimates vary, and the slide should say so). 3 Sourcing note: who named the Trail of Tears, and who named the Era of Good Feelings (outlined 10/11).

Activity 24 min

Case discussion, day 2. Question 2: if you were advising the members of Congress who opposed removal, what would you tell them to do? Question 3: will the Senate ratify, and what does the answer say about American democracy in the 1830s? Tally the vote slips on the board before the reveal.

Closure 7 min

Thesis, revised: rewrite your Day 1 sentence with this week's evidence. Does the Cherokee case support, modify, or refute what you wrote? Collected with the discussion materials. Then five to know cold, pairs quizzing each other aloud: spoils system, universal white manhood suffrage, the Bank veto, nullification, Indian Removal Act.
