The course description (CED)

The College Board's AP U.S. History Course and Exam Description (PDF, effective Fall 2026). It is the official list of what the exam covers: nine units, eight themes, six skills. Every topic number on a day page points into it.

The eight themes and their key questions
NAT American and National Identity
What did this say about who Americans are, or what the nation stands for?
WXT Work, Exchange, and Technology
How did it involve money, trade, work, or technology?
GEO Geography and the Environment
How did land, resources, or region shape it?
MIG Migration and Settlement
Who moved or was moved, and why?
PCE Politics and Power
Who gained or lost power? What did government do, and who argued about it?
WOR America in the World
How were other nations or peoples involved?
ARC American and Regional Culture
What beliefs, ideas, or creative expression shaped it or came out of it?
SOC Social Structures
Which groups gained, lost, or were left out?
